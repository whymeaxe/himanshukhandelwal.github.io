4. How consent works

  • The first time you visit, a banner asks you to Accept analytics or Decline. Both choices are equally easy. Until you choose, analytics is off.
  • If you decline, Google Analytics is never loaded and no analytics cookie is set.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, I treat it as a decline and do not show the banner. You can still opt in through "Cookie settings".
  • You can change or withdraw consent at any time through Cookie settings. If you withdraw, analytics stops and I remove the analytics cookies that I can access. Withdrawing does not affect anything done before you withdrew.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
